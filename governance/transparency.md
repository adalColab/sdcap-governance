# Transparency Guidelines

**What's public, what's not, and why.**

---

## Our Commitment

As a 501(c)(3) nonprofit serving a community built on Radical Inclusion and Communal Effort, we default to transparency. Information is shared unless there's a specific reason to protect it.

---

| Information | Where to Find |
|-------------|---------------|

| Information | Why Protected |
|-------------|---------------|

| Individual donor amounts | Donor privacy |

| Security procedures | Safety |

| Investigation details | Due process |
| Individual member issues | Privacy |

## Financial Transparency

### We Publish:
- Annual budget (summary and major categories)
- Year-end financial statements
- 990 tax returns
- Grant awards (recipient, purpose, amount)
- Major expenditure categories
- Audit results (if applicable)

### We Protect:
- Individual donor names (unless they consent)
- Individual donation amounts
- Bank account details
- Pending grant applications
- Salary/compensation details (aggregate OK)

### Upon Request:
- Detailed line-item budget (to members)
- Specific expenditure documentation (with legitimate purpose)
- Audit working papers (to board only)

---

## Decision Transparency

For every significant decision, we document:

1. **What** was decided
2. **When** it was decided
3. **Who** voted and how
4. **Why** (rationale, at least summary)
5. **What happens next** (implementation)

These records are:
- Stored permanently
- Available to members
- Summarized publicly

---

## Handling Information Requests

### From the Public:
1. Acknowledge within 48 hours
2. Provide 🟢 Public information immediately
3. For 🟡/🔴 information, explain why it's protected
4. Offer alternatives if possible

### From Members:
1. Acknowledge within 24 hours
2. Provide 🟢 and 🟡 information promptly
3. For 🔴 information, explain protection and escalate to board if needed

### From Media:
1. Route to designated spokesperson
2. Provide only 🟢 Public information
3. Coordinate response with board chair

## Proactive Disclosure

We don't wait to be asked. We actively publish:

- **Monthly:** Upcoming events, meeting dates
- **Quarterly:** Financial summary, project updates
- **Annually:** Full annual report, 990, impact summary

---

## Whistleblower Protection

If you observe something that seems wrong:

1. **Report** to any board member or via anonymous form
2. **No retaliation** — we protect reporters absolutely
3. **Investigation** — taken seriously, handled confidentially
4. **Resolution** — reported back to you (if not anonymous)

---

## When Transparency Conflicts

Sometimes transparency values conflict:

| Conflict | Resolution |
|----------|------------|
| Privacy vs. Accountability | Anonymize where possible, disclose patterns |
| Safety vs. Openness | Safety wins, explain limitation |
| Speed vs. Process | Document after the fact, explain urgency |
| Confidentiality vs. Member Interest | Escalate to full board for guidance |

When in doubt: **Ask.** Consult the board chair or relevant committee.

---

## Transparency Failures

If we fail to be transparent when we should have been:

1. **Acknowledge** the failure publicly
2. **Explain** what happened
3. **Correct** by disclosing the information (if appropriate)
4. **Prevent** by fixing the process

No coverups. No spin. We're human, we make mistakes, we own them.
