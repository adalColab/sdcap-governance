# Board Rules of Order

**Structured procedures for effective, participatory board meetings.**

---

## Meeting Principles

1. **Respect everyone's time** — Start on time, end on time
2. **One voice at a time** — No cross-talk
3. **Assume good faith** — We're all here for the mission
4. **Decide, don't defer** — Action over endless discussion
5. **Document everything** — If it's not written, it didn't happen

---

## Meeting Structure

### Standard Board Meeting Agenda

| Time | Item | Lead |
|------|------|------|
| 0:00 | Call to Order & Roll Call | Chair |
| 0:05 | Approval of Previous Minutes | Secretary |
| 0:10 | Consent Agenda | Chair |
| 0:15 | Treasurer's Report | Treasurer |
| 0:25 | Committee Reports | Committee Chairs |
| 0:45 | Old Business | Chair |
| 1:00 | New Business | Chair |
| 1:30 | Open Forum (Community) | Chair |
| 1:45 | Announcements | All |
| 1:55 | Next Meeting & Adjournment | Chair |

*Total: 2 hours maximum*

---

## Consent Agenda

Items that require board approval but no discussion:

- Routine approvals
- Committee appointments
- Policy renewals
- Previously discussed items ready for vote

**Process:**
1. Chair reads consent agenda items
2. Any member may pull an item for discussion
3. Remaining items approved by single motion
4. Pulled items move to New Business

## Motion Process

### Making a Motion

1. **Member:** "I move that [specific action]."
2. **Another Member:** "I second."
3. **Chair:** "It has been moved and seconded that [action]. Is there discussion?"

### Discussion Rules

- Speak once before others speak twice
- Stay on topic
- Time limit: 2 minutes per speaker (Chair may extend)
- Chair may call for "any further discussion?" to close debate

### Voting

1. **Chair:** "All in favor say 'aye.'"
2. **Chair:** "All opposed say 'nay.'"
3. **Chair:** "Abstentions?"
4. **Chair:** "The motion [passes/fails] [vote count]."

### Vote Types

| Type | When Used | Threshold |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| Voice Vote | Routine matters | Majority present |
| Show of Hands | When voice unclear | Majority present |
| Roll Call | Financial > $1000, bylaws, contested | Recorded individually |
| Unanimous Consent | Obvious consensus | No objections |

## Quorum

- **Required:** Majority of voting board members
- **If no quorum:** Discussion only, no binding votes
- **Virtual attendance:** Counts toward quorum if video-enabled

---

## Officer Roles in Meetings

### Chair
- Calls meeting to order
- Maintains order and focus
- Recognizes speakers
- States motions and results
- Breaks ties (if bylaws permit)
- Does NOT make motions (passes gavel to Vice Chair first)

### Vice Chair
- Acts as Chair in their absence
- Receives gavel when Chair wishes to make a motion
- Assists with procedural matters

### Secretary
- Records attendance
- Takes minutes
- Reads previous minutes
- Tracks action items
- Distributes minutes within 48 hours

### Treasurer
- Presents financial report
- Speaks to budget implications of motions
- Certifies funds available for approved expenditures

## Special Procedures

### Point of Order
**Member:** "Point of order!"  
**Chair:** "State your point."  
**Member:** "The motion is out of order because [reason]."  
**Chair:** Rules on the point (or refers to bylaws).

### Point of Information
**Member:** "Point of information?"  
**Chair:** "State your question."  
*Used to ask factual questions, not to debate.*

### Call the Question
**Member:** "I call the question."  
*Requires second and 2/3 vote to end debate immediately.*

### Table a Motion
**Member:** "I move to table this item."  
*Postpones indefinitely. Requires majority vote.*

### Refer to Committee
**Member:** "I move to refer this to [Committee] for recommendation."  
*Delegates research/recommendation. Returns at next meeting.*

## Executive Session

Used for:
- Personnel matters
- Legal issues
- Real estate negotiations
- Sensitive member issues

**Process:**
1. Motion to enter executive session (state reason)
2. Non-board members and relevant staff excused
3. No minutes of discussion (only actions taken)
4. Motion to exit executive session
5. Any votes taken in executive session re-stated in open session

---

## Virtual Meeting Protocols

- Video on when speaking
- Mute when not speaking
- Use "raise hand" feature
- Chat for queue and clarifications only
- Screen share for documents under discussion
- Recording requires advance notice and consent

---

## Conflict of Interest

If a member has a conflict:
1. Disclose the conflict before discussion
2. Answer factual questions if asked
3. Leave the room during discussion and vote
4. Abstention noted in minutes

---

## Amendment to These Rules

These rules may be amended by:
- 2/3 vote of the board
- With 7 days written notice
- Effective immediately upon passage

---

*Based on Robert's Rules of Order, simplified for accessibility.*
