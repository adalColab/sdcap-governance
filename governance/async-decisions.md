# Async Decision Protocol

**How we make decisions between meetings.**

---

## Why Async?

We're volunteers. We have jobs, families, burns to prepare for. Requiring everyone in the same room at the same time for every decision slows us down. Async decisions allow us to move faster while maintaining transparency and participation.

---

## When to Use Async vs. Meeting

### Use Async Decision Protocol For:
- Routine approvals within established policy
- Time-sensitive matters that can't wait
- Simple binary decisions (approve/deny)
- Matters already discussed in committee
- Expenditures under $500 within budget

### Require Full Board Meeting For:
- Bylaw changes
- Officer elections
- Expenditures over $5,000
- New partnerships or contracts
- Policy changes
- Matters with significant disagreement
- Anything a member requests be held for meeting

---

## The Async Process

### Step 1: Proposal Submission

**Proposer** posts to designated async channel (Discord/Slack/email) with:

```
📋 ASYNC DECISION REQUEST

Title: [Clear, specific title]
Proposed By: [Name]
Decision Deadline: [Date/time - minimum 72 hours]

Background:
[Why this is needed]

Proposal:
[Specific action requested]

Impact:
[Budget, timeline, affected parties]

Recommendation:
[What you're asking the board to approve]
```

### Step 2: Discussion Period (48 hours minimum)

- Any board member may ask questions
- Proposer responds to clarifications
- Members may suggest amendments
- Proposer may modify proposal based on feedback

### Step 3: Voting Period (24 hours)

After discussion closes, voting opens:

| Vote | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| ✅ | Approve as written |
| ❌ | Oppose (must state reason) |
| 🔄 | Request modification |
| ⏸️ | Hold for meeting (blocks async) |
| ➖ | Abstain (still counts for quorum) |

### Step 4: Resolution

**Passes if:**
- Quorum of board members vote
- Majority vote ✅
- No ⏸️ (hold for meeting)

**Fails if:**
- Majority vote ❌
- Quorum not reached
- Any member votes ⏸️

**Modified if:**
- Majority vote 🔄
- Proposer accepts modifications
- New vote called on modified proposal

---

## Quorum for Async

- Same as in-person: Majority of voting board members
- Abstentions count toward quorum
- No response ≠ abstention (does not count)

---

## Emergency Decisions

For truly urgent matters (< 48 hours):

1. **Chair** declares emergency and reason
2. Discussion period: 12 hours minimum
3. Voting period: 6 hours minimum
4. Requires 2/3 approval (not simple majority)
5. Ratification required at next regular meeting

---

## Documentation

All async decisions must be:

1. **Recorded** in official minutes of next meeting
2. **Filed** in decision log with:
   - Date approved
   - Vote count
   - Full proposal text
   - Implementation responsibility
3. **Announced** to relevant stakeholders

---

## Appeals

Any member may appeal an async decision:

1. Submit appeal within 48 hours of result
2. State grounds (procedural error, new information, etc.)
3. Matter moves to next board meeting
4. Original decision stands until board rules on appeal

---

## Best Practices

### For Proposers:
- Be specific — vague proposals get questions, not votes
- Provide context — assume not everyone has background
- Set realistic deadlines — don't rush unless necessary
- Respond promptly — delayed answers delay decisions
- Accept feedback gracefully — modifications aren't rejection

### For Voters:
- Read the full proposal
- Ask questions during discussion, not voting
- If you need more time, ask (don't just ignore)
- If opposing, explain why
- If you can't evaluate, abstain with reason

### For Everyone:
- Keep discussion professional
- One thread per proposal
- No side conversations
- Assume good faith
- Celebrate decisions made, even if you disagreed

## Platform-Specific Guidelines

### Discord
- Use dedicated #board-async channel
- Use threads for each proposal
- React with vote emoji
- Pin active proposals

### Email
- Subject: [ASYNC] Proposal Title
- Reply-all for discussion
- Clear VOTE: header for votes
- Secretary tallies and announces

### Loomio / Similar
- Follow platform's native proposal format
- Use built-in voting features
- Set automatic close date

## Example Async Decision

```
📋 ASYNC DECISION REQUEST

Title: Approve Q1 Newsletter Design Contract
Proposed By: Communications Committee
Decision Deadline: Friday 5pm PT

Background:
We've been working with Designer X on newsletter templates.
They've completed the design phase and we need to approve
payment for the next phase.

Proposal:
Approve payment of $450 to Designer X for Q1 newsletter
template finalization, per the approved communications budget.

Impact:
- Budget: $450 from Communications line item ($1200 remaining)
- Timeline: Work begins upon approval, delivery in 2 weeks
- Affected: Communications Committee, all subscribers

Recommendation:
Motion to approve $450 payment to Designer X for newsletter
template work.

---

Discussion:
[Board Member A]: Is this within the preapproved vendor list?
[Proposer]: Yes, Designer X was approved in October.
[Board Member B]: Looks good to me.

Votes:
✅ Member A
✅ Member B  
✅ Member C
✅ Member D
➖ Member E (abstain - conflict of interest)
✅ Member F

Result: APPROVED 5-0-1
Recorded by: Secretary, [Date]
```
