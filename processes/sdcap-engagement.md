# SDCAP Engagement Process

**How to work with the nonprofit.**

---

## What is SDCAP?

**San Diego Collaborative Arts Project** is the 501(c)(3) nonprofit that:
- Holds fiscal responsibility
- Provides tax-deductible donation pathways
- Manages grants and fiscal sponsorship
- Maintains insurance and liability coverage
- Stewards organizational knowledge

SDCAP is the **OXYGEN** in our Fire Triangle — we enable, we don't own.

## Engagement Types

### Information Requests

**What:** Questions about community, resources, history, how things work

**How:** Just ask — Discord, email, at events

**Timeline:** Usually answered within 48 hours

**No formal process needed.**

---

### Event Support

**What:** You're organizing an event and need help

**Options:**
- Insurance certificate
- Permitting guidance
- Promotion through SDCAP channels
- Equipment/supplies
- Volunteer coordination
- Financial processing

**Process:**
1. Contact Events Committee (events@[domain])
2. Describe event: what, when, where, who, how many
3. Specify what support you need
4. Committee responds within 72 hours
5. Formal agreement if significant resources involved

**Timeline:** Request 30+ days before event for best results

---

### Fiscal Sponsorship

**What:** Your project needs a 501(c)(3) to receive grants or tax-deductible donations

**Requirements:**
- Aligned with SDCAP mission (arts, community, 10 Principles)
- Clear project scope and timeline
- Responsible project lead(s)
- Budget and financial tracking capability
- Willingness to follow SDCAP reporting requirements

**Process:**
1. Submit Fiscal Sponsorship Application
2. Review by Finance Committee (14 days)
3. Board approval if significant scope
4. Sign fiscal sponsorship agreement
5. SDCAP provides EIN and donation processing
6. Quarterly reporting required

**Fees:** Typically 5-10% of funds processed (covers overhead)

**→ Use: [Project Proposal template](../templates/project-proposal.md)**

---

### Grant Applications

**What:** You want funding from SDCAP's grant programs

**Current Programs:**
- Art Grants (project-based)
- Community Development Grants
- Emergency Assistance
- *(Programs vary by year and funding)*

**Process:**
1. Check if grants are open (announced via website/Discord)
2. Submit grant application by deadline
3. Grants Committee reviews
4. Notification within 30 days of deadline
5. If approved: sign grant agreement, receive funds, deliver project
6. Submit completion report

**→ Watch announcements for application periods**

---

### Project Proposals

**What:** You have an idea for something SDCAP should do or support

**What makes a good proposal:**
- Clear problem or opportunity
- Specific solution
- Defined scope (what's in, what's out)
- Resource requirements (money, time, people)
- Alignment with mission and 10 Principles

**Process:**
1. Draft proposal using template
2. Submit to relevant committee
3. Committee review and feedback
4. Revision based on feedback
5. Committee recommendation to board (if needed)
6. Board decision
7. Implementation begins

**→ Use: [Project Proposal template](../templates/project-proposal.md)**

---

### Technology/Infrastructure

**What:** You want to improve or add organizational systems

**Philosophy:**
```
INTRODUCE → PARALLEL → TRANSITION
```

1. **Introduce:** Pilot the new system
2. **Parallel:** Run alongside existing (prove it works)
3. **Transition:** Replace if proven, sunset if not

**Process:**
1. Submit technology proposal
2. Tech Committee evaluation
3. Pilot approval
4. Parallel period (duration varies)
5. Transition decision

**→ Use: [Technology Proposal template](../templates/technology-proposal.md)**

## The "Yes... And!" Promise

When SDCAP can't directly support something, we will:

1. **Acknowledge** — "Yes, this sounds valuable"
2. **Explain** — Why it's not a fit for us to own
3. **Enable** — Here's how you can make it happen
4. **Connect** — Here's who might help
5. **Offer** — What support we *can* provide

**You will never hear just "no."**

## Responsibilities

### SDCAP Commits To:
- Respond to all requests
- Explain decisions clearly
- Connect you with alternatives if we can't help

- Maintain transparency about our capacity
- Celebrate community successes

### You Commit To:
- Align with 10 Principles
- Follow through on commitments
- Communicate proactively
- Accept that "not now" isn't "never"
