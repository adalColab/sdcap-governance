# Fire Party 🔥

**A short working session to keep momentum directed (fire) instead of noisy (heat).**

---

## What Is a Fire Party?

A 45–60 minute recurring session that does three things:

1. **Sense** — Read the signals
2. **Stabilize** — Clarify and assign
3. **Strengthen** — Push work forward

It's not a status meeting. It's a working session. Things move during Fire Party.

---

## Cadence

- **Weekly** during active phases
- **Biweekly** during maintenance phases
- **On-demand** before events or deadlines

---

## Inputs (Before the Party)

Gather these before starting:

- [ ] New issues since last party
- [ ] Stale issues (no touch in 14+ days)
- [ ] Upcoming dates, events, milestones
- [ ] Blockers flagged by anyone

---

## Agenda (45 Minutes)

### 1. Sense (10 min)

**Question:** What is the actual shape of things?

- Scan new issues
- Ask: What's the real problem? Who's affected?
- Quick-tag with `fire:*` and `growth:*` labels

Don't solve yet. Just see.

### 2. Stabilize (20 min)

**Question:** What needs to lock down?

For each priority item:

| Do This | Why |
|---------|-----|
| Confirm stage (`sense` / `stabilize` / `strengthen`) | Know where it is |
| Define "done" | Know what success looks like |
| Define smallest next step | Know what happens tomorrow |
| Assign owner OR mark `help-wanted` | Know who's got it |
| Set Next Touch date | Know when to check back |

If something can't stabilize, it needs more sensing. That's okay.

### 3. Strengthen (15 min)

**Question:** What can we push forward *right now*?

- Pick 1–3 items to advance today
- Open PRs, create sub-issues, or draft content
- Pair up if helpful

This isn't "plan to do later." This is "do right now together."

---

## Outputs (Always)

Every Fire Party produces:

| Output | Where |
|--------|-------|
| Labels applied | On each issue |
| Owners / Next Touch set | In project board |
| Recap posted | In the Fire Party thread |

### Recap Format

Post a short summary:

```
🔥 Fire Party [Date]

Moved:
- Issue #X → stage:stabilize (owner: @name)
- Issue #Y → merged!

Blocked:
- Issue #Z needs decision from [committee]

Needs Heat:
- Issue #W is ready but unclaimed
```

---

## Who Attends?

- **Required:** At least one person with merge/assign permissions
- **Welcome:** Anyone who wants to contribute
- **Optional:** Stakeholders for active issues

Fire Party works with 2 people. It scales to 6. More than that, split into working groups.

---

## Fire Party vs. Regular Meetings

| Fire Party | Regular Meeting |
|------------|-----------------|
| Working session | Discussion/decision session |
| Move issues forward | Make organizational decisions |
| Open to anyone | May have restricted attendance |
| 45 min, focused | Variable length |
| Produces artifacts | Produces minutes |

They complement each other. Fire Party clears the path. Meetings decide direction.

---

## Tips

- **Time-box ruthlessly** — If an item needs more than 5 minutes, create a sub-issue and move on
- **Celebrate closes** — Merged PRs and resolved issues deserve acknowledgment
- **Name blockers explicitly** — "Blocked on X" is useful; "stuck" is not
- **Rotate facilitation** — Share the load, spread the context
- **Keep the energy up** — Put on music, bring snacks, make it a party
