# Community Workflow 🔥🐾

**How we run community work: transparent, asynchronous, documented.**

---

## Philosophy

We run community work like modern open source:

- **Transparent by default** — decisions have receipts
- **Asynchronous by default** — people contribute when they can
- **Documented by default** — our Story stays in-house

This is how we keep our narrative in our own hands while enabling others to build.

---

## Shared Language

Our work uses three interlocking frameworks to stay legible and focused.

### 🔥 Fire Triangle (What's Missing?)

Every piece of work needs resources from the triangle. Label each issue:

| Label | Element | Meaning |
|-------|---------|---------|
| `fire:fuel` | 🟡 FUEL | Resources, tools, space, materials |
| `fire:oxygen` | 🔵 OXYGEN | Info, logistics, process, enablement |
| `fire:heat` | 🔴 HEAT | People, energy, participation, momentum |

### 📈 Growth Path (What Kind of Thing?)

How mature is this work? Where does it belong?

| Label | Stage | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| `growth:project` | PROJECT | Bounded deliverable with clear end |
| `growth:party` | PARTY | Time-boxed collaboration session |
| `growth:practice` | PRACTICE | Ongoing maintenance, ritual, stewardship |

*Start small → Prove it works → Institutionalize what earns it*

### 🔄 3S Stage (How Does It Mature?)

Track where work is in its lifecycle:

| Label | Stage | Focus |
|-------|-------|-------|
| `stage:sense` | SENSE | Discover the real shape, gather signals |
| `stage:stabilize` | STABILIZE | Bound scope, pick owners, reduce wobble |
| `stage:strengthen` | STRENGTHEN | Ship, document, automate, teach others |

## Where Things Live

| Place | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| **Issues** | Todos, proposals, decisions, bugs |
| **Discussions** | Open-ended conversation, Q&A, announcements |
| **Pull Requests** | Edits to docs, code, standards |
| **Projects** | The live board (stage + owner + next touch) |

## The Freeforall Rule (With Structure)

**Anyone can open an issue.**

Maintainers do lightweight triage:

- Apply labels
- Set next touch date
- Close with "Yes… And" if not a fit

No permission needed to propose. No gatekeeping on ideas.

---

## "Yes… And" Closure

When we close or decline something, we respond with:

1. **Acknowledge** — "Yes, this sounds valuable"
2. **Explain** — Why we can't own it right now
3. **Enable** — How you can still make it happen
4. **Connect** — Who or what might help
5. **Offer** — What support we *can* provide

**You will never hear just "no."**

## How to Participate

### Adding a Freeforall Todo

1. Go to **Issues → New issue**
2. Pick **Freeforall Todo**
3. Fill in one sentence + smallest next step
4. Submit

Optional: comment "I'll take point" to claim it.

### Claiming Work

No permission drama. Just comment:

> "I'll take point. Next step: ____. Rough timing: ____."

If you can't self-assign, maintainers assign during Fire Party.

### Making Edits

1. Open the file on GitHub
2. Click **Edit** (pencil icon)
3. Commit to a new branch
4. Open a Pull Request
5. PR gets reviewed and merged
