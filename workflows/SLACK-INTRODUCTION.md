## Talking Points (For Video Call or In-Person)

### Why This? (2 min)

- We had ideas scattered across Discord, email, and people's heads
- Hard to know what was active, blocked, or done
- Wanted async-friendly patterns so volunteers can contribute anytime
- Built on open source community management best practices

### The Three Frameworks (3 min)

**Fire Triangle** 🔥
> "Every piece of work needs Fuel (resources), Oxygen (info/process), and Heat (people). When something's stuck, ask: which element is missing?"

**Growth Path** 📈
> "Is this a bounded Project, a time-boxed Party, or an ongoing Practice? Start small (project), prove it works (party), then institutionalize what earns it (practice)."

**3S Stages** 🔄
> "Work matures through Sense → Stabilize → Strengthen. Don't ship something you haven't scoped. Don't scope something you don't understand."

### Fire Party Demo (5 min)

Walk through one cycle:

1. **Sense** — "Here are 3 new issues. Let's quick-tag them."
2. **Stabilize** — "This one needs an owner and a next step."
3. **Strengthen** — "This one is ready to ship. Let's do it right now."

Show how an issue moves across the board.

### Participation Model (2 min)

- Anyone can open a Freeforall Todo
- No permission drama to claim work — just comment "I'll take point"
- If we decline something, we use "Yes... And" — never just "no"

### Q&A Prompts

- "What kinds of things would you drop into a Freeforall Todo?"
- "What's blocked right now that a Fire Party could unstick?"
- "Any concerns about the workflow?"

## FAQ (Anticipated Questions)

**"Is this replacing our existing processes?"**
> No — this complements formal governance. Fire Parties handle everyday work; board meetings handle binding decisions.

**"Do I need to learn all of this to participate?"**
> Nope. Just open a Freeforall Todo. The labels and triage happen in Fire Party.

**"What if I don't use GitHub?"**
> Fire Party handles the GitHub side. You can participate via Discord/Slack for discussion, and others will translate to issues.

**"How is this different from what we do now?"**
> More explicit stages, clearer ownership, and a dedicated working session (not just async chat).
