## Philosophy Note

These additions implement a "GitHub as arts reactor" model — using issues, discussions, projects, and PRs as the shared ground for transparent, async-first community work. The Fire/Growth/3S labeling system keeps work legible across repositories and teams.

The Fire Party ritual provides structured momentum without heavy meeting overhead, embodying the "Yes... And" promise by keeping ideas moving rather than blocking them.
